How to Connect 3 Bluetooth Devices on Windows

Windows 10 and 11 hardware supports pairing and maintaining simultaneous active connections to multiple Bluetooth audio devices. SoundSync Multi-Out bridges the Windows audio engine to stream sound to all of them together!

1

Pair 1st Bluetooth Device

Open Windows Settings (Win + I) > Bluetooth & devices. Put your first headphones/speaker into pairing mode and click Add device.

2

Pair 2nd Bluetooth Device

Put your second Bluetooth headphone or speaker into pairing mode. Click Add device again and connect. Both devices will show as Connected audio.

3

Pair 3rd Bluetooth Device

Put your third Bluetooth headset/speaker into pairing mode and connect. Windows keeps all 3 connected simultaneously!

4

Click "Refresh Devices" & "Start Broadcasting"

In SoundSync Multi-Out, click Refresh Devices. Enable the toggles for all 3 devices and press START BROADCASTING. Audio from any app (YouTube, Spotify, etc.) will play across all devices in real-time!

5

Sync Latency & Control Individual Volumes

Different Bluetooth brands have slight wireless delays. Use the Latency Sync slider on each card to dial out any echo, and adjust independent volume sliders to each listener's preference.
