How to Connect 3 Bluetooth Devices on Windows

Step-by-step wireless audio routing guide

1
Pair 1st Bluetooth Headphones

Press Win + I to open Windows Settings ➔ Bluetooth & devices. Put your first earbuds/headphones into pairing mode, click Add device, and connect.

2
Pair 2nd Bluetooth Headphones / Speaker

Put your second Bluetooth device into pairing mode. Click Add device again and connect. Windows keeps both active!

3
Pair 3rd Bluetooth Device

Put your third Bluetooth headphones into pairing mode and connect. Windows will show all 3 devices connected simultaneously.

4
Click "Refresh" & "Start Broadcast" in SoundSync

Click Refresh in SoundSync Multi-Out. All 3 devices will show up with Bluetooth badges. Enable them and press START BROADCAST!

5
Calibrate Wireless Latency Sync

Different Bluetooth brands (AirPods, boAt, OnePlus, Sony) have different wireless latencies. Use the Sync Delay slider on each channel to eliminate any echo.
